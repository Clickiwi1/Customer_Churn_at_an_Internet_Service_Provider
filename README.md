# Customer Churn at an Internet Service Provider
Analysis of Analysis of Customer Churn at an Internet Service Provider | SQL · Excel · Tableau/PowerBI | SQL · Excel · Tableau/PowerBI


Conducted a full cycle of data analysis (7,043 customers, California, Q3) to identify the causes of churn and develop a customer retention strategy for senior leadership (CEO, Vice President, Marketing Department).

Key Findings:

Identified the main factors contributing to churn: monthly contracts (45.8% of churn), fiber-optic service (41%), and lack of online security (31.3%)
Switching from a monthly contract to an annual contract reduces churn by a factor of 4.3 (from 45.8% to 10.7%)
Actual financial losses were calculated: $139,000 per month, $1.67 million per year
A strategy to retain 20–30% of churning customers saves $330,000–$500,000 per year
